# Tapas 0.1.1 — A home in your Dock

Tapas now appears in the Dock and opens a normal **Your plate** window. Open it
from Applications, Spotlight or the Dock to reach Dictado, Acta, Recent and
Preferences—even when a crowded MacBook menu bar hides the pintxo.

- Reopening Tapas brings its home window forward instead of returning to setup.
- First-launch setup can be postponed and resumed from inside the app.
- Completing the walkthrough is remembered as soon as the final screen is reached.
- Existing downloaded models load even if the old setup’s final button was missed.
- The menu-bar icon stays compact while recording and remembers its chosen position.
- Closing a window keeps Tapas running. Use **Quit Tapas** to quit.

## Update

Choose **Check for Updates…**, or download **Tapas-0.1.1-arm64.dmg** and replace
Tapas in Applications after finishing any recording and quitting the old copy.
Settings, models and transcripts are preserved. Requires Apple silicon and macOS
15 or later. The app and installer are Developer ID signed and notarized.
