# tapas 0.2.4

Clearer voice model setup and automatic finishing for suggested meeting recordings.

- **Understand model setup:** see which model is being checked, downloaded or prepared for your Mac. Downloads show a percentage; local preparation shows a spinner and elapsed time instead of appearing stuck at 20%.
- **Know what to expect:** setup explains that first-time preparation can take several minutes and asks you to keep tapas open. Preparation errors include the underlying error message, and downloaded files are kept for retrying.
- **Finish suggested recordings automatically:** Acta recordings started from a meeting suggestion finish when the meeting app releases the microphone. Manually started recordings remain under your control.

This update explains model preparation more clearly; it does not change the model or remove macOS's first-time preparation work.

Choose **Check for Updates…** in tapas, or download **tapas.dmg** and drag tapas into Applications. Settings, recordings and transcripts are preserved.

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon. Powered by Desert Ant Labs.
