# tapas 0.3.0

A new look for all of tapas: Sobremesa.

- **One card from the menu bar:** click the pintxo to open what you kept. Recordings are told apart by their first words, whether they were a meeting or a take, how long they ran, their languages, and their shape: you above the line, others below.
- **House rules on the back:** turn the card over to change how tapas behaves. Every setting is a sentence. Click the keys to record a new shortcut, slide a piece onto its pick to turn something on, and install the skill for Claude Code, Codex or Cursor only if you want to.
- **A new welcome:** five short steps. The speech model downloads while you allow access, try a practice take with your own shortcut, meet Acta, and read your house rules.
- **Acta, printed:** the floating pintxo listens to both sides of a call, with you on the left and everyone else on the right. Pausing sits it down; the controls never change size. The call suggestion asks simply: Keep this call?

Your recordings, folder, shortcuts and settings are unchanged.

Choose **Check for Updates…** in tapas, or download **tapas.dmg** and drag tapas into Applications.

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon. Powered by Desert Ant Labs.
