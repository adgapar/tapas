# tapas 0.2.6

A new look for Dictado.

- **A printed pintxo that listens:** while you speak, the pick lies flat and the four pieces stand up on it, growing with your voice. When you finish, they lie back down on the tilted pick and settle with “Listo”.
- **Live words you can read:** your words appear in a paper card set in Fraunces, with the newest words marked in saffron.
- **Same behavior:** the signal still hangs below the menu bar and camera, never changes size during a take, and Finish and Cancel still appear when you hover or focus it. Shortcuts, history and pasting are unchanged.

Choose **Check for Updates…** in tapas, or download **tapas.dmg** and drag tapas into Applications.

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon. Powered by Desert Ant Labs.
