# Tapas 0.1.4

A new welcome and a smaller home for your tools.

- **Enter the bar:** an inward-opening door leads to a menu on the counter, followed by guided setup and a receipt when you are ready.
- **A compact tool receipt:** Dictado and Acta stay within reach in a smaller home window, which expands when you open a tool.
- **Clearer Preferences:** aligned switches, grouped shortcut selectors, and compact buttons in the Tapas style.
- **Consistent artwork:** the detailed pintxo identity now carries through the app and README, with a smoother transition into the recording waveform.

Choose **Check for Updates…** in Tapas, or download the DMG and drag Tapas into Applications.

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon. Powered by Desert Ant Labs.
