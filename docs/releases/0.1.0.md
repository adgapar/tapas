# Tapas 0.1.0

**Small tools. Good company.**

Local dictation and meeting transcripts for Mac.

- **Dictado:** press Control–Option, speak, and put your words into the app you were using.
- **Acta:** capture microphone and meeting-app audio, pause and resume, and save a timestamped Markdown transcript.
- **Your files:** saved transcripts live in `~/Documents/tapas/`, ready for your editor, scripts and AI tools.
- **Automatic updates:** future releases arrive through the app.

## Install

Download **Tapas-0.1.0-arm64.dmg** and drag Tapas into Applications. If replacing an
older copy, finish recording and quit Tapas first, then choose Replace. Eject the
DMG and open Tapas from Applications. Your models, settings and transcripts stay
intact. Requires Apple silicon and macOS 15 or later.

The installer opens with just Tapas and Applications on a Gráfico background.
The app and DMG are Developer ID signed, notarized by Apple and stapled.
Existing Preview 2 installations can also choose **Check for Updates…**.

## Current scope

Acta produces transcripts; generated summaries, action items and individual
speaker identification are not included. Selecting a browser can capture audio
from its other tabs. Live-call accuracy and audio-device changes need broader
testing; please report issues. Privacy and license notices are included in the app.

The DMG and checksum are the installation assets. Current source and packaging
instructions are available in the repository; see the
[build and signing guide](https://github.com/adgapar/tapas/blob/main/docs/SIGNING.md).
