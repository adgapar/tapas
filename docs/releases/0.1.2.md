# Tapas 0.1.2

A shared home for your thoughts and conversations.

- A floating home with equal space for Dictado and Acta, recent transcripts, and shared Preferences. Open the same home from the Dock or menu bar; return from either tool with All tools.
- A four-step first-launch welcome with Pintxo, clear permission actions, a native folder picker, and a private practice take. Finish later and resume where you left off.
- A separate **Control–Shift–M** shortcut opens Acta. Change either shortcut in Preferences; conflicting combinations are rejected.
- Choose one transcript folder for both tools. Existing files stay in place, and active or recovered recordings retain their original destination.
- Meeting invitations work before setup is complete and remain while the app uses your microphone. Recording starts only after you choose it. Ready users can accept without leaving their call.
- Click the floating Pintxo for recording controls. Its ingredients become an audio-reactive waveform and gather again on pause or save. Reduced Motion keeps the mark still.
- Acta explicitly asks for meeting-audio access and explains macOS’s Screen & System Audio Recording permission. Granting access never starts a recording.

Apple silicon · macOS 15 or later. Drag Tapas into Applications, or use the built-in updater. Existing settings and transcripts are retained.

Acta uses local microphone activity as a hint; it cannot identify an exact browser meeting tab. Selecting a browser can include audio from other tabs. It produces timestamped source-labeled transcripts; summaries and individual speaker identification are not included.
