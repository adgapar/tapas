# tapas 0.2.3

Choose your assistant and start with editable templates during onboarding.

- **Assistant selection:** explicit buttons for Claude Code, Codex and Cursor replace the dropdown in onboarding and Preferences.
- **Editable templates:** use **Open templates** during onboarding or in Preferences to open starter Markdown templates for meeting notes, customer interviews and project updates. Existing custom templates are preserved.
- **Assistant playbooks:** the tapas skill includes workflows for meeting notes, polished dictation, weekly reviews and more.
- **Consistent downloads:** the public installer is now available as `tapas.dmg`; versioned installers remain available for automatic updates.

Choose **Check for Updates…** in tapas, or download **tapas.dmg** and drag tapas into Applications.

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon. Powered by Desert Ant Labs.
