# tapas 0.2.5

Faster on-device transcription on supported Macs.

- **Faster Dictado and Acta transcription:** updated Desert Ant Labs Voz runtime overlaps recognition work on Apple silicon. The gain varies by Mac; the speech model and its existing download are unchanged.
- **Faster filler detection:** the updated Uhm model uses a new artifact. Tapas may download and prepare it on first launch after the update. Existing transcripts and settings are preserved.

Choose **Check for Updates…** in tapas, or download **tapas.dmg** and drag tapas into Applications. There is no need to clear the model cache.

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon. Powered by Desert Ant Labs.
