# Tapas 0.1.5

A quieter Acta, ready for your next conversation.

- **Start without an app picker:** Acta captures your microphone and computer audio. Other apps and notification sounds can be included; screen images are never saved.
- **Stay in the meeting:** starting a recording closes the main window and leaves the floating Pintxo, with small pause/resume and finish controls below it.
- **A waveform that follows the conversation:** eight slim bars respond to recent audio levels, then gather back into the Pintxo during silence.
- **A simpler finish:** saved meetings show a short receipt. Reopening Acta prepares the next recording; viewing or exporting the previous transcript is an explicit choice, with actions kept above the text.
- **More reliable Arc suggestions:** microphone activity from Arc’s browser helpers now matches the main app correctly.

Existing transcripts and recovery files remain readable.

Choose **Check for Updates…** in Tapas, or download the DMG and drag Tapas into Applications.

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon. Powered by Desert Ant Labs.
