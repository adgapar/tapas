# Tapas 0.2.0

Your recordings, ready for your AI assistant.

- **Find and cite your saved words:** install the Tapas skill for local Claude Code, Codex, or Cursor from Preferences → Use with your AI assistant. Terminal is optional; start a new assistant session after installing.
- **Browse a growing library:** INDEX.md links recent recordings and monthly archives, with dates, durations, and short previews. Tapas refreshes indexes automatically and offers a manual rebuild.
- **Consistent transcript metadata:** new Dictado and Acta files include stable recording IDs, capture details, language information, and redaction status. SCHEMA.md explains the format and audio-source labels.
- **Change folders without losing discovery:** one location setting points assistants to the current transcript folder and previously selected locations. Existing files stay where they are.
- **Keep control of your files:** original transcripts and edited indexes are preserved. Skill updates and removal protect user edits. Claude Code and Codex installations can coexist; Cursor reuses a compatible copy.

No new AI models or automatic summaries are added. Dictado history remains redacted; Acta keeps the full transcript. Your assistant still needs permission to read files, and its provider settings apply to any text it processes. Remote assistants cannot automatically read this Mac.

Choose **Check for Updates…** in Tapas, or download the DMG and drag Tapas into Applications.

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon. Powered by Desert Ant Labs.
