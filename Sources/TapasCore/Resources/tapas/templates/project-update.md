# Project update

Use for a concise update based on one or more recordings.
The section guidance below describes what to write; do not copy it into the result.

## Progress
Summarize reported progress with source citations. Distinguish plans from completed work.

## Decisions and changes
Explain confirmed changes in direction and their stated reasons.

## Blockers
List unresolved blockers and dependencies; distinguish older reports from current evidence.

## Next steps
List agreed actions, owners, and dates where recorded. Mark unknowns explicitly.
