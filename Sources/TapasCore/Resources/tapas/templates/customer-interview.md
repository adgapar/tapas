# Customer interview

Use for customer research. Separate observations from your interpretation.
The section guidance below describes what to write; do not copy it into the result.

## Context
Describe the customer's situation and goals supported by the recording.

## Findings
Group needs, pain points, and workarounds. Include short supporting quotes
with source citations. Do not infer how common a finding is from one interview.

## Opportunities
Describe possible responses as suggestions, not customer commitments.

## Follow-up questions
Capture unanswered questions worth investigating.
